Telangana

India's Youngest State • Deccan Plateau Heritage & Modern Vibrance

About Telangana

In the heart of India's Deccan Plateau, Telangana welcomes travellers with a blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Officially designated as the newest and youngest state within India on June 2, 2014, Telangana boasts an incredible heritage spanning centuries.

[Hyderabad](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/telangana), its bustling capital, is a city of contrasts where historic landmarks like the iconic [Charminar](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/telangana) and majestic [Golconda Fort](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/telangana) stand alongside glitzy tech hubs and modern malls.

Beyond the city's limits, lush forests, serene lakes, ancient temples like the UNESCO-listed [Ramappa Temple](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/telangana), and a warm culinary legacy featuring the world-famous Hyderabadi Biryani await every explorer.

Top Attractions in Telangana

Charminar, Hyderabad

The iconic 16th-century monument and mosque featuring four grand minarets, standing as the historic heart of Hyderabad.

📍 Open Live Directions

Thousand Pillar Temple, Warangal

A stellar masterpiece of Kakatiya architecture built in 1163 CE, famous for intricately carved rock pillars and rock-cut shrines.

📍 Open Live Directions

Ramappa Temple (UNESCO Site)

A remarkable UNESCO World Heritage medieval Hindu temple showcasing floating lightweight bricks and stunning Kakatiya artistry.

📍 Open Live Directions

Golconda Fort, Hyderabad

A majestic medieval fortress complex renowned for its acoustic engineering, grand palaces, and historic diamond trading heritage.

📍 Open Live Directions
HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Art, Crafts & Dance

Home to the dynamic Kakatiya-era dance form *Perini Sivatandavam*, alongside rich handicraft traditions like Bidriware metal craft, Dokra castings, and Nirmal arts.

🍛 Hyderabadi Gastronomy

A celebrated culinary paradise featuring the world-famous Hyderabadi Dum Biryani, aromatic Haleem, rich kebabs, and Deccani delicacies.

🎉 Major Festivals

The state celebrates vibrant cultural festivals including **Bathukamma** (the enchanting floral festival during Dasara), Bonalu, and religious events.
