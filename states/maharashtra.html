About Maharashtra

Maharashtra, a state steeped in compelling narratives, stands as a testament to the richness of India's varied heritage and the grandeur of its landscapes. It is a land where history echoes through ancient landmarks, and diverse traditions thrive in harmony.

The state takes pride in its wealth of historical wonders that reflect the grandeur of bygone eras, featuring 5 UNESCO World Heritage Sites. From the renowned [Ajanta](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/maharashtra), [Elephanta](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/maharashtra), and [Ellora Caves](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/maharashtra), showcasing ancient rock-cut architecture, to the architectural brilliance of Mumbai's landmarks.

From the bustling financial capital of Mumbai and the majestic hill stations of Mahabaleshwar to the spiritual abodes of Nashik and Shirdi, Maharashtra offers an extraordinary journey into the heart of India.

The Gateway of India, Mumbai

Ellora Caves, Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar

Elephanta Caves

A UNESCO World Heritage collection of cave temples carved from solid rock on an island off the coast of Mumbai, dedicated to Lord Shiva.

A historically significant landmark in Pune linked deeply to Mahatma Gandhi and the Indian freedom movement.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Land of Saints & Maratha Legacy

Renowned as the "Land of Saints" and steeped in Maratha history, Maharashtra preserves a vibrant legacy of hill forts, folk art, Lavani dance, and timeless heritage.

🍛 Maharashtrian Gastronomy

A flavorful culinary tapestry featuring iconic street foods like Vada Pav and Misal Pav, alongside Puran Poli, Modak, and zesty regional thalis.

🎉 Major Festivals

The state celebrates grand cultural events with unmatched fervor, most notably the vibrant **Ganesh Chaturthi** festival, Gudi Padwa, and traditional folk celebrations.
