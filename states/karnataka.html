About Karnataka

Situated in the embrace of the Western Ghats and stretching its arms towards the Arabian Sea, the state of Karnataka beckons travellers with a symphony of landscapes, cultures, and history.

This enchanting state, known for its heritage, wildlife, silk, spices, and sandalwood, unfolds like a treasure trove of experiences waiting to be discovered.

From the magnificent royal splendor of Mysuru Palace and the ancient stone poetry of Hampi to the tech dynamism of Bengaluru and the pristine coastal beaches of Gokarna, Karnataka offers an unforgettable southern expedition.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Art, Craft & Coffee

Karnataka is India's largest coffee producer (accounting for 70% of national production), renowned for intricate Hoysala temples, Bidriware metal craft, and silk traditions.

🍛 Karnataka Gastronomy

Savour a rich culinary symphony featuring Bisi Bele Bath, Mysore Bonda, Mangalore Buns, Neer Dosa, and authentic filter coffee.

🎉 Major Festivals

Celebrated with unmatched grandeur, key annual spectacles include **Mysuru Dasara**, Kadalekai Parishe, Kaveri Sankramana, and Hampi Utsav.
