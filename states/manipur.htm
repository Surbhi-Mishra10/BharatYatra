About Manipur

Tucked away in the northeastern corner of India lies a land of enchantment, Manipur. Often referred to as the "Switzerland of India" due to its stunning landscapes, lush green hills, and picturesque valleys, this hidden gem is a captivating blend of nature's bounty, rich artistic traditions, vibrant festivals, and delectable cuisine.

The state is famously home to **Loktak Lake**, the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, known for its floating islands called "phumdis" and as the natural habitat of the endangered dancing deer—the **Sangai deer**—found nowhere else in the world.

Stepping into Manipur feels like entering a time capsule where age-old traditions coexist harmoniously with modernity, highlighted further by its distinction as the birthplace of modern polo (*Sagol Kangjei*).

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Art, Polo & Heritage

Manipur is the historic birthplace of modern polo (*Sagol Kangjei*) and classical Manipuri dance, alongside rich handloom traditions and unique arts displayed at the Manipur State Museum.

🍛 Manipuri Cuisine

A healthy and aromatic culinary experience featuring traditional specialties like Eromba, Singju, Morok Metpa, and bamboo shoot-infused regional dishes.

🎉 Major Festivals

The state celebrates grand cultural events including the **Sangai Festival** (held in November), the joyous harvest **Kut Festival**, Lai Haraoba, and Yaoshang.
