About Haryana

The term 'Haryana' originates from two Sanskrit words, 'Hari' (God) and 'ayana' (home), symbolizing a land embraced by divine heritage. It epitomises the contemporary face of India, embracing industrial and agricultural progress while staying deeply rooted in its ancient traditions.

Steeped in history, Haryana was the legendary battleground for the epic Mahabharata war at Kurukshetra as well as the historic decisive battles of Panipat. From ancient Vedic settlements to thriving urban hubs like Gurugram, Haryana bridges historical legacies with forward-looking modern innovation.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Folk Arts & Crafts

Haryana boasts energetic folk dance traditions like Ghoomar, Loor, and Dhamal. The state is also internationally famed for its handloom traditions and rural handicrafts showcased widely during events like the Surajkund Mela.

🍛 Traditional Cuisine

Known for wholesome rustic flavors, authentic Haryanvi cuisine features dairy-rich preparations including fresh white butter (Makhan), Bajre ki Roti, Churma, Kichri, and traditional milk delicacies like Rabri.

🎉 Major Festivals

Festivals are celebrated with immense community fervor, including Gita Mahotsav in Kurukshetra, Baisakhi, Teej (welcoming the monsoon), Gugga Naumi, and regional harvest celebrations.
