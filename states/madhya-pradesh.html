About Madhya Pradesh

In the heart of India lies a realm where history whispers through ancient corridors, where nature's beauty reigns supreme, and where vibrant cultures flourish in harmony with the land. Madhya Pradesh, a state steeped in captivating narratives, stands as a testament to the richness of India's heritage and the magnificence of its diverse landscapes.

The state proudly boasts 3 UNESCO World Heritage sites: the mystical temples of Khajuraho, the sacred aura of [Sanchi Stupa](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/madhya-pradesh/bhopal/sanchi-stupa), and the ancient artistry preserved in [Bhimbetka Rock Shelters](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/madhya-pradesh/bhopal/bhimbetka-rock-shelters).

Madhya Pradesh is also widely celebrated as the **'Tiger State of India'**, featuring expansive national parks and wildlife sanctuaries like Bandhavgarh and Panna where majestic tigers roam freely.

Top Attractions in Madhya Pradesh

Khajuraho Group of Monuments

A world-renowned UNESCO World Heritage site celebrated for its exquisite Nagara-style architecture and intricate erotic and spiritual stone carvings.

📍 Open Live Directions

Sanchi Stupa

A sacred Buddhist complex commissioned by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE, featuring magnificent ornamental gateways (toranas).

📍 Open Live Directions

Gwalior Fort

Described by Mughal Emperor Babur as "the pearl among fortresses in Hind," featuring the vibrant blue-tiled Man Mandir Palace.

📍 Open Live Directions

Bandhavgarh National Park

A prime tiger reserve boasting one of the highest densities of Royal Bengal Tigers in India, set amidst dense Sal forests and ancient hills.

📍 Open Live Directions
HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Classical Arts & Heritage

The cultural cradle of musical legends like Tansen, Madhya Pradesh hosts the prestigious **Khajuraho Dance Festival**, Tansen Samaroh in Gwalior, and rich tribal art traditions.

🍛 Malwa & Tribal Cuisine

A flavorful gastronomic heritage featuring Malwa specialties like Poha-Jalebi, Bhutte Ka Kees, Dal Bafla, kebabs, and rich regional sweets.

🎉 Major Festivals

The state celebrates grand events including the Khajuraho Dance Festival, Lokrang Festival in Bhopal, Bhagoria festival among tribal communities, and Ujjain Singhasta Kumbh.
