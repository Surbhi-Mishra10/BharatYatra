About Tripura

Located deep within the northeastern part of India, the state of Tripura is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travellers. This enchanting state is a harmonious blend of breathtaking landscapes, vibrant cultures, and rich heritage.

From the tranquil beauty of its lush green valleys and rolling hills to the intricate craftsmanship of its handlooms and handicrafts, Tripura offers an array of unique experiences. Explore ancient temples that resonate with spiritual energy and historic palaces reflecting royal grandeur.

Whether you're an adventurer seeking trekking trails through dense forests or a history buff eager to unravel the mysteries of rock carvings like Unakoti, Tripura promises a journey of wonder and discovery.

Top Attractions in Tripura

Ujjayanta Palace, Agartala

A magnificent former royal palace in the heart of Agartala featuring stunning tiled floors, curved wooden ceilings, and beautiful Mughal-style gardens.

📍 Open Live Directions

Neermahal Water Palace

India’s unique water palace situated right in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake, blending Hindu and Islamic architectural styles.

📍 Open Live Directions

Tripura Sundari Temple, Udaipur

One of the 51 revered Shakti Peethas in India, drawing thousands of devotees to this ancient spiritual landmark.

📍 Open Live Directions

Unakoti Rock Carvings

An ancient hillside archaeological wonder featuring colossal rock-cut sculptures and stone images of Hindu deities.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Art & Handlooms

Celebrated for exquisite bamboo and cane handicrafts, traditional handloom weaving, and vibrant tribal dance forms reflecting indigenous roots.

🍛 Traditional Cuisine

Savour authentic Tripuri gastronomy featuring dishes like *Mui Borok*, flavored with local herbs, fermented fish (*Berma*), and organic bamboo shoots.

🎉 Major Festivals

The state celebrates grand cultural gatherings including the Kharchi Puja, Garia Puja, Ker Puja, and vibrant Ashokastami celebrations at Unakoti.
