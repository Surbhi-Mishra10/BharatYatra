About Gujarat

Welcome to the vibrant state of Gujarat, where the past and present dance in harmony, and every corner whispers tales of bygone eras and vibrant traditions. Extending gracefully into the Arabian Sea, and boasting a sprawling coastline that spans 1,600 kilometres, Gujarat proudly stands as the home state of Mahatma Gandhi, Father of the Nation.

From majestic temples and architectural marvels to diverse traditions and natural beauty, this western Indian state is a treasure trove of experiences, beckoning travellers to embark on a journey that spans centuries.

Home to the world's tallest statue (the [Statue of Unity](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/gujarat)), India's first UNESCO World Heritage City ([Ahmedabad](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/gujarat)), and the only natural habitat of Asiatic lions in Gir, Gujarat offers an unforgettable expedition.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Art, Crafts & History

Birthplace of the ancient Indus Valley Civilization sites like Lothal and Dholavira, renowned for intricate Rogan art, Bandhani textiles, and exquisite woodwork.

🍛 Gujarati Gastronomy

A delightful culinary experience featuring iconic Gujarati Thali specialties, Dhokla, Fafda, Khandvi, Undhiyu, and sweet delicacies like Jalebi.

🎉 Major Festivals

Celebrated with unmatched enthusiasm including the grand **Rann Utsav**, vibrant **Navratri Garba Mahotsav**, Uttarayan (International Kite Festival), and Modhera Dance Festival.
