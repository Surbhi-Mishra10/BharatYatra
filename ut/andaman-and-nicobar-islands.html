About Andaman and Nicobar Islands

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands provide an idyllic blend of palm-fringed beaches, white sand stretches extending for miles, unperturbed serenity, and crystal-clear turquoise waters that epitomize a tropical paradise. Situated in the Bay of Bengal, this destination comprises nearly 300 islands.

While some islands have been developed for tourism purposes, others remain uninhabited to preserve their untouched natural beauty. Whether you're seeking lively shores, delicious seafood delights, or tranquil coastal escapes immersed in nature, this destination has something for everyone.

From exploring historic colonial landmarks in Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair) to discovering unique natural wonders like mud volcanoes on Baratang Island, the archipelago offers unforgettable island adventures.

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Island Heritage & Tribes

Home to ancient indigenous communities alongside a harmonious multicultural immigrant population blending coastal traditions, music, and arts.

🍛 Coastal Delicacies

A paradise for seafood lovers, offering fresh catches of fish, crabs, lobsters, and tropical island culinary treats.

Celebrated with coastal enthusiasm, including the annual Island Tourism Festival, beach carnivals, Durga Puja, and national holidays.
