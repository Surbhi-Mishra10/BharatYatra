About Jammu and Kashmir

Often celebrated as the crown of India, Jammu and Kashmir is a breathtaking symphony of snow-capped Himalayan peaks, verdant valleys, glistening lakes, and cascading rivers.

From the spiritual sanctity of Jammu's shrines to the romantic houseboats and floating gardens of Srinagar's Dal Lake, and the world-class ski slopes of Gulmarg, the region offers an enchanting escape across every season.

Beyond its famous tourist landmarks lie untouched wonders like Gurez Valley, lush saffron fields, and historical Mughal gardens that echo tales of timeless elegance.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Arts & Crafts

Renowned worldwide for exquisite Pashmina shawls, intricate hand-knotted silk carpets, walnut wood carving, papier-mâché crafts, and vibrant Kashmiri folk dances like Rouf.

🍛 Culinary Traditions

Indulge in royal culinary feasts like the multi-course Wazwan, authentic Kahwa tea infused with saffron and almonds, Rogan Josh, and traditional breads from local bakeries (Kandur).

🎉 Major Festivals

Key celebrations include the Tulip Festival in Srinagar, Shivratri (Herath), Hemis (in neighboring Ladakh influences), Urs celebrations at historic shrines, and the Vaishno Devi Festival.
