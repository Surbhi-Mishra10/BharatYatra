About Chandigarh

Imagine a city where wide boulevards lined with lush greenery intersect with meticulously planned architecture, set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Shivalik mountain range.

Envisioned by the renowned Swiss-French architect Le Corbusier, Chandigarh is India's first planned city. It features a unique grid-like design, well-defined sectors, serene lakes, and expansive green parks.

As a vibrant union territory serving as the joint capital of Punjab and Haryana, Chandigarh blends modern urban infrastructure with rich cultural heritage.

HERITAGE & CELEBRATIONS

Culture, Art & Festivals

🎭 Art & Architecture

Chandigarh is a world-renowned canvas of modernist urban design. It hosts thriving art galleries like the Government Museum and Art Gallery, open-air art exhibitions, and a vibrant theatre culture at Tagore Theatre.

🍛 Local Cuisine

Influenced by rich Punjabi traditions, local delicacies include Makki di Roti with Sarson ka Saag, Butter Chicken, Chole Bhature, and street food favorites in Sector 17 and Sector 22 markets.

🎉 Major Festivals

The city comes alive during the annual Rose Festival (held in February), Mango Festival, Baisakhi celebrations, Lohri bonfires, and vibrant cultural events at the Open Hand Monument.
